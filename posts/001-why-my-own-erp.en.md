# Why I started building my own ERP

A retrospective based on my recollections. The exact starting date has not been reconstructed; publication date: 6 October 2026 (Asia/Shanghai).

I wanted to build a temporary stopgap for the warehouse. It began growing into my own ERP — a system for managing resources and operations.

## The problem

The business was growing, and goods began going missing in the warehouse. Our existing manual controls were no longer enough.

We started moving to 1C. In our case, the implementation felt slow to me. Meanwhile, the gaps in warehouse operations needed attention.

## The decision

I decided to try a simple temporary solution with GPT’s help. I wanted to cover the immediate gaps while implementation continued. My own ERP came into the story later — at first, I needed a stopgap.

## What we did

I started with a temporary solution and iterations with GPT: trying things, checking them, and returning to improvements. Gradually, the solution began developing into an ERP.

I lead the development with GPT’s help: I explain the task, look at the result, and come back with clarifications.

## The result as I saw it

The result pleasantly surprised me: the temporary solution began growing into a system. For me, that changed the scope of the idea itself.

Implementation is still underway. Step by step, we are checking what we built in development in everyday use.

## What proved difficult

Testing raised questions, and implementation brought difficulties that required further work. Getting a result in development and bringing it into everyday use proved to be different stages.

I will leave the details of specific errors for a separate, verified account.
