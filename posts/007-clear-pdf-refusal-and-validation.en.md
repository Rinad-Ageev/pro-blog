## What an unclear refusal makes difficult

When document preparation stops without an understandable reason, it is hard to tell what needs attention. This is a general operational problem, not an account of a particular user incident. I am not reconstructing an exact error or the circumstances that caused it.

## The boundary I would preserve

I would separate an understandable refusal from permission to obtain the document. An explanation helps someone investigate. By itself, it should not permit a download while the data remains unverified.
