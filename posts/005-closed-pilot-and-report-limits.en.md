## When can clients be admitted?

The client portal has been deployed. Is that enough to entrust it with real client data? I propose examining that question through a saved report about one closed pilot.

## How to read the pilot status

A closed owner pilot is a limited stage. That label alone does not confirm that a client completed a full working scenario. A decision about client access needs a clear account of what was actually checked.

## What was deployed

According to the saved report, the client portal was deployed as a closed owner pilot on 28 September. The published interface still required verification of real data sources, permissions, and data exchange with the ERP.

This is a separate historical milestone. It does not describe the portal's current state or establish continuous operation since that date.

## What the report supports

This account rests on a dated deployment report. No fresh check of the running system was conducted for this entry. The documented result supports a statement about the pilot deployment within the report's scope. It does not prove a full client workflow.

## What remained unverified

Real client records and access were not created during the reported pass. Real data sources, client permissions, and ERP exchange still required separate verification. This account does not confirm who completed a working scenario or what a client accepted.

## What I would propose checking next

Before an authorized test, I would separately record the data source, the permitted role and its access, the expected ERP exchange, and the evidence required for each item. I would then compare expectations with observations. This is a proposal for a future check, not a completed test or a description of a ready integration.
